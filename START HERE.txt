THE ENCHANTED EVENING FATHER–DAUGHTER BALL · SPONSOR BANNER
HOW TO PLAY IT ON THE TV (no internet needed)
================================================

Everything the banner needs is inside this folder. Keep the folder
together — don't move files out of it — and it will play from a laptop,
a USB stick or the desktop with no internet connection at all.


BEFORE THE EVENT (5 minutes, do this at home)
---------------------------------------------
1. Copy this whole folder onto the laptop you'll use (the desktop is fine).
2. Open the folder and double-click   index.html
   It should open in Chrome or Microsoft Edge. If it opens in something
   else, right-click index.html → "Open with" → Chrome or Edge.
3. You'll see the banner in a frame with a small row of controls under it.
   Let it run for a minute to confirm the slides change on their own.
4. Close it. You're ready.


ON THE NIGHT
------------
1. Plug the laptop into the TV with the HDMI cable and turn the TV to
   that HDMI input.
2. Set the laptop to EXTEND its screen to the TV (not mirror):
     Windows:  press  Windows key + P  → choose "Extend"
     Mac:      System Settings → Displays → turn OFF "Mirror"
3. Double-click  index.html  to open the banner.
4. Drag the browser window across onto the TV.
5. Click  Fullscreen  (bottom-right of the controls) — or press the  F  key.
   The banner now fills the TV and runs by itself all evening.
6. Turn on Do Not Disturb on the laptop so no pop-ups appear on the TV,
   plug the laptop into power, and leave it.

   It loops all evening on its own and keeps the screen awake by itself.
   As a backup, before guests arrive:
     • Laptop: keep it PLUGGED IN and the LID OPEN.
         Windows: Settings → System → Power → "When plugged in, turn off
                  my screen after" → Never (and "put my device to sleep
                  after" → Never).
         Mac:     System Settings → Lock Screen → "Turn display off on
                  power adapter when inactive" → Never.
     • TV: many TVs switch themselves off after a few hours without a
       remote button press. In the TV's settings look for "Auto Power
       Off", "Eco" or "Sleep Timer" and turn it OFF.
     • Don't minimise the banner window or switch to another tab in it:
       it keeps the screen awake only while it is showing.


THE CONTROLS (if you need them)
-------------------------------
   F              fullscreen on / off          (Esc also exits fullscreen)
   P              pause / resume
   → and ←        next / previous slide        (Space also goes forward)
   Home           jump back to the title page
   M              switch between the cream "Evening" look and the dark
                  "Midnight" look — pick whichever suits the room lighting.
                  (The sun/moon icon under the banner does the same.)
   The same keys work while fullscreen. Move the mouse and faint arrows
   appear in the corners of the screen too.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
• Black bars around the picture?  The TV isn't 16:9 or the window isn't
  on the TV yet. Drag the window onto the TV and press F again.
• Banner blank or no pictures?  A file was moved out of the folder.
  Re-copy the whole folder from the USB stick.
• Controls missing / only the banner shows?  You opened banner.html
  instead of index.html. That's fine too — press F for fullscreen.
• It opened in Safari / Internet Explorer and looks odd?  Use Chrome or
  Edge instead.
